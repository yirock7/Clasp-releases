Clasp 0.4.2 finishes a fix from 0.4.1.

## Fixed

- **The "Pair in Windows Settings instead" button in the welcome guide no longer looks cut off when you point at it.** Buttons grow slightly when you point at them, and the guide's page was trimming the part of this button that grew past its left edge, so one end looked flattened. 0.4.1 changed the button's highlight but not this. The page now leaves room at its edges, so buttons and their keyboard focus outlines show in full.

## Installing

Download `Clasp-0.4.2-win-x64-setup.exe` and its `.sig` file into the same folder, and **check the signature first** (steps in the README). Windows will probably show a "Windows protected your PC" box because the installer is not code-signed yet; after the signature check passes, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If you already have Clasp, click **Update** in Settings instead.
