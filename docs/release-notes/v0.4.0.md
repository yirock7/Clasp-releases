Clasp 0.4.0 makes first-time setup easier: you can pair your AirPods from the welcome guide, and pick your main settings before you finish.

## What's new

- **Pair from the welcome guide.** While the pairing page is open, Clasp looks for AirPods and Beats that are in pairing mode and lists them with a **Pair** button. Nothing pairs until you click, so a neighbour's AirPods in pairing mode are never picked up by accident. After pairing, Clasp selects your AirPods and connects them if Windows hasn't already. If your headphones ask for a code, or they don't show up, the **Pair in Windows Settings instead** link still works as before.
- **Quick settings in the welcome guide.** A new last page lets you choose your low-battery alert level and how Clasp behaves when your AirPods connect: connect automatically when nearby, switch sound to them, play music when they connect, and pause when they disconnect. The defaults are the same as before, and all of these are still in Settings.

## Installing

Download `Clasp-0.4.0-win-x64-setup.exe` and its `.sig` file into the same folder, and **check the signature first** (steps in the README). Windows will probably show a "Windows protected your PC" box because the installer is not code-signed yet; after the signature check passes, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If you already have Clasp, click **Update** in Settings instead.
