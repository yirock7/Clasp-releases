Clasp 0.6.1 fixes the "Last seen" time in the flyout and tidies a few things behind the scenes. It is still a preview release, and the source code is public.

## Fixed

- **"Last seen" now matches the battery readings on show.** The flyout could say "Last seen 37 min ago" or even "1 day ago" while your earbuds and case showed readings from a few minutes earlier. Clasp only counted sightings it was extra sure about for that line. It now follows the same sightings as the battery rows, is never older than the newest reading on screen, and keeps ageing while the flyout stays open.
- **A crash is written to `hub.log` once instead of twice.**

## Behind the scenes

- The credits now lead with [LibrePods](https://github.com/kavishdevar/librepods), whose research Clasp is built on, followed by CAPod and MagicPodsCore. You can see them in the README, THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and Settings > About.
- A test that occasionally failed in CI, and the installer build's start-up check, were made reliable.

## Installing

Download `Clasp-0.6.1-win-x64-setup.exe` and its `.sig` file into the same folder, and **check the signature first** (steps in the README). Windows will probably show a "Windows protected your PC" box because the installer is not code-signed yet; after the signature check passes, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If you already have Clasp, click **Update** in Settings instead.
