Clasp 0.4.1 fixes three things you could see in 0.4.0.

## Fixed

- **AirPods no longer jump back into the case while you're wearing them.** The picture in the tray flyout first showed your AirPods out of the case, then switched to them sitting in it, even though nothing had changed. Clasp now keeps the in-ear picture for as long as your AirPods keep reporting that they're in your ears.
- **Battery history is readable when you don't use your AirPods with this PC every day.** Long stretches with no readings now shrink to a narrow break mark, so the times you did use them get enough room to read. Each stretch is labelled with when it started. A single charging reading no longer shows as a thin full-height green bar. Clasp still never fills in battery levels it didn't actually see.
- **The "Pair in Windows Settings instead" link no longer looks cut off** when you point at it in the welcome guide.

## Installing

Download `Clasp-0.4.1-win-x64-setup.exe` and its `.sig` file into the same folder, and **check the signature first** (steps in the README). Windows will probably show a "Windows protected your PC" box because the installer is not code-signed yet; after the signature check passes, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If you already have Clasp, click **Update** in Settings instead.
